## What this is

### Resuming after a limit (checked 2026-10-30)
- **What works:** a session can schedule its own wake-up with the claude-code-remote tools, with nothing for Adam to send. `send_later` (a one-shot) and `create_trigger` (a cron or a one-shot bound to this session) deliver a prompt into the same conversation when it is idle; a prompt due during a running turn waits and is delivered after that turn ends; delivery survives container restarts (the tools' own contract). Tried at the setup: a `send_later` of three minutes (`trig_01NxkBExBrisG5tymz4CPXeG`, fire time 16:10 UTC) was registered and listed by `list_triggers`; its delivery is the first turn of this session after the setup turn ended (the reply "resume test delivered", visible in the session's transcript).
