## Process (BUILD.md 2.1, step 4)

Written from the brief's description of BUILD.md 2.1 (the file stays out of the repo and its text did not reach the setup session); the rules themselves are in RULES.md ("Escalation", "Reviewer rules", "Verification by chunk type", "Usage", "Decision cards"). Where this section and BUILD.md disagree, BUILD.md wins and this section is corrected.

- **Roles and models.** The coordinator (Opus 5.5, max) runs the build from this file: it starts each builder and each reviewer itself (a subagent can't start a subagent here), merges nothing itself, and keeps CONTEXT.md and HISTORY.md. A **builder** (`.claude/agents/builder.md`, Sonnet 5.5, max) builds one chunk on `build/chunk-<N>` off `staging` and reports. A **DATA chunk** gets **its own fresh reviewer** (`.claude/agents/reviewer-data.md`, Opus 5.5, max): full review, the builder's break proofs re-run, the staging check, the merge, the record. **LIGHT chunks** are built **two or three at a time** (a batch: chunks that share no files and depend on nothing unmerged, each on its own branch), then **one reviewer for the batch** (`.claude/agents/reviewer-light.md`, Sonnet 5.5, high) reviews them together, merges them in the batch's order and runs one smoke. No day mode, no night mode, no per-chunk mode: one process.
- **Branches and merges.** One PR per chunk into `staging`, squash-merged by its reviewer; never stacked; every chunk leaves staging usable; a migration chunk runs alone and its migration is live on staging (the deploy read, the probe green) before any chunk that reads it starts; nothing touches `master` before the release.
- **Decision cards.** A builder or reviewer that meets something SPEC leaves open or contradicts, a prototype state that contradicts SPEC, or a change beyond its scope does not route around it: it writes a decision card (RULES.md, "Decision cards") into its report and stops the chunk only when the card blocks the merge; the coordinator puts the card to Adam between chunks and records his answer as a DECISIONS.md entry (and a SPEC.md line when it changes behaviour or wording). DECISIONS.md is a record, not an inbox.
- **Stop rules.** A builder stops at a blocking card, at a shared-script change (`check-migration.mjs`, `migration-rules.mjs`, `check-context-size.mjs`, the staging guards), at anything that would write production or read another person's data, and at its budget (RULES.md, "Usage"). A reviewer merges only a chunk whose behaviour is right; a behaviour failure goes back to the builder with the exact failing case; a missing proof (behaviour verified right, a test missing) is merged and logged as an owed test for the next chunk that touches the file, never bounced; the second behaviour failure of one chunk stops the build for Adam. A usage-limit stop is resumed by the coordinator's own scheduled task (CONTEXT.md, "Resuming after a limit"), with nothing for Adam to send.
- **Verification by chunk type** (RULES.md has the rule): a DATA chunk break-proves every place that writes (one break each) and gets a staging check; a LIGHT chunk gets one break per call pattern, its screens captured at 390 px from fixtures, and no per-chunk click-through. **Screens are compared with the prototype once, before the release** (BUILD.md step 5, "Before the release"), by the final reviewer, not after every chunk. Fixtures tell the right data source from the wrong one; each chunk's Verification lists writers **W** and display wiring **D** and ends with what it would not catch.
- **Screens.** Every screen chunk commits `design/1.2.1/built/<DEMO state>.png` + `.txt`; the one comparison with the prototype's own screenshots (`prototype/check/run.sh` writes them to `prototype/check/shots/`) is for the same elements, order and wording, never pixel-matched. Data handling is never lifted from the prototype: a `useDemo` read, `demo/seed.ts`'s counts or `removeRun` in a real file fails the review.
- **New screens use tokens only** (SPEC "Not this version") and `--on-accent` on the accent; every new screen file joins `REBUILT_SCREENS` in `textOnAccent.static.test.ts`; no new wording says meso, run, tag or "change the plan" (`check-wording.test.mjs` on every chunk; DECISIONS 251).
- **Live checks on staging** are the reviewer's, as the staging accounts, with Playwright and the bypass header (never printed). Two checks can't write what they need on staging and are proven differently: maintenance on (the replay for the refusal, route interception for the screens; DECISIONS 254) and an old app version (a local build at 1.2.0 against staging's database; chunk 73).
- **Usage** (RULES.md, "Usage"): narrow reads, the affected tests while building, the full suite once at the end, batched turns, quiet reporters; every chunk report carries its context figure and its cost-weighted total (DECISIONS 267).
- **Release:** 1.2.1 the 1.2 way (section "Release"); the first release under BUILD.md step 5 in full is 1.2.2's.
